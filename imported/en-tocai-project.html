The project is conducted by ISISLab within the National Interuniversity Consortium for Computer Science (CINI). The project partners (besides CINI) are: CM Sistemi s.p.a., SELEX Sistemi Integrati, National Research Council, Think3 Inc.

In particular, ISISLab activities are carried out under the coordination of Domenico Talia in Activity 10: Grid and service-oriented platforms for distributed collaborative environments. The goal is to define Grid systems and distributed platforms to support the management of data and users in distributed collaborative environments with the study of authentication, authorization and integrity issues related to data access and use of multi-channel communication platforms to improve interaction and exchange of information in the context of collaborative working.
