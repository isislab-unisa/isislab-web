Regional Center of Competence for the Development and Transfer of Innovation Applied to Cultural and Environmental Heritage.

The Regional Center of Competence for Development and Transfer of Innovation Applied to Cultural and Environmental Heritage was created by the Campania Region and a large part of the regional public research network operating on the theme of Cultural and Environmental Heritage. Together, Campania Region and CNR, University of Campania, Science and Technology Parks, European University Center for Cultural Heritage have invested economic resources for about 29,000,000.
