ISISLab professors and researchers are directly involved in the teaching activity of the Master degree of Science in Computer Science of the University of Salerno, in particular as regards the Cloud Computing address (held in Italian ).

The Cloud Computing address is organized (2019-2020 Academic year) on three fundamental teachings in the first year:

  • Cloud Computing (Vittorio Scarano) (First semester)
  • Reti Geografiche: struttura, analisi e prestazioni (Delfina Malandrino) (First semester)
  • Programmazione Concorrente, Parallela e su Cloud (Vittorio Scarano / Carmine Spagnuolo) (Second semester)

    plus some courses chosen as regards the first and second year.

    "The Cloud Computing curriculum in less than 2 minutes!"
