Bachelor’s degree programs

ISISLab professors and researchers are directly involved in the teaching activity of the Bachelor's Degree (in Italian, Corso di Laurea Triennale) .

Master’s degree programs in Computer Science

ISISLab professors and researchers are directly involved in the teaching activity of the Master degree of Science in Computer Science of the University of Salerno, in particular as regards the Cloud Computing address (held in Italian ).

Follows the organization for the year 2024-25 for Cloud Computing.

  • Serveless computing for IOT (Vittorio Scarano)
  • Architetture distribute per il cloud (Gennaro Cordasco)
  • Reti Geografiche: struttura, analisi e prestazioni (Delfina Malandrino)
  • Programmazione Concorrente, Parallela e su Cloud (Carmine Spagnuolo)
  • Cloud Computing (Maria Angela Pellegrino)
  • High performace computing (Biagio Cosenza)
  • Reti ad hoc (Biagio Cosenza)
