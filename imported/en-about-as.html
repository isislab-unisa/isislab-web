ROUTE-TO-PA project

The results of the ROUTE-TO-PA project have been selected by the Commission to be placed in the Innovation radar www.innoradar.eu.

The Innovation Radar is a European Commission initiative to identify high potential innovations and innovators in EU-funded research and innovation projects. The overall goal of Innovation Radar is to allow every citizen, public official, professional and business person to discover the outputs of EU innovation funding and give them a chance to seek out innovators.

The innovations recognized are:

  • A new open data platform for enhancing transparency of Public Administration and for offering a friendly and accessible environment to public administrators, organizations and citizend to access, reuse and publish open data (based on SPOD and TET). See the Innovation Radar at:  https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873 
  • An open source and browser-based software for creating and analysing decision trees, based on SilverDecisions. See the Innovation Radar at:  https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875 

The recognizion of our project results is very positive and underlines the quality of the work done by the whole team, during and after the project!
