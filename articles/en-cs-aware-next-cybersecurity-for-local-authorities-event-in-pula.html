On January 24, 2025, Pula's Municipal Library Conference Room hosted an Info Day for the CS-AWARE-NEXT project, focusing on enhancing cybersecurity in local governments 🏛️💻.
The event featured presentations on the project's objectives and tools 📊🛠️, followed by discussions on implementing effective cybersecurity strategies in public administrations 🛡️📌.
This initiative is part of a series of outreach activities aimed at improving digital resilience across Sardinian municipalities 🌐🔒.
