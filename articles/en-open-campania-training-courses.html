In the first two weeks of April 2019, laboratories for the "Open Data Campania" project, led by prof. Vittorio Scarano, University of Salerno were organized in Naples (Italy), at the Palazzo Armieri, Campania Region.

Through a series of short presentations and personal workshop activities, participants will discover how it is possible to create datasets and visualizations. For the creation of datasets, in particular, the principles of agile design have been illustrated, as well as the continuous control of the usability of the dataset, together with the guide to effective meta-dating by DCAT-AP_IT. Eventually, each participant will have covered the entire production cycle of a dataset, until the final publication on a CKAN server.
