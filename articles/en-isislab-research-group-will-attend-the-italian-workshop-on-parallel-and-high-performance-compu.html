Carmine Spagnuolo and Biagio Cosenza will present the research group of the ISISLab laboratory of the Computer Science Department (University of Salerno) at the following workshop:

"Italian Workshop on Parallel and High Performance Computing Technologies", Bologna, September 5th, 2019

The detailed program with 30 presentations related to research groups and research topics in the area of high-performance parallel computing is available at the following link: https://sites.google.com/a/unipi.it/itwshpc19/program4:56 PM

Cosenza Biagio, UNISA, Celerity: High-level C++ for Accelerator Clusters
Spagnuolo   Carmine, UNISA, Presentation of research team
