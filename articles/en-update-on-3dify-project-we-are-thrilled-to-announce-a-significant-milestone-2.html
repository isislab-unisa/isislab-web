🎉 We have officially reached the alpha phase of release 1.0 of 3Dify, our innovative tool for automatic 3D model generation from a single photo. This marks a crucial step forward in our commitment to making 3D processing more accessible and user-friendly for everyone.

💻 To make our progress more transparent and collaborative, we have released the source code on the project's GitHub repository: https://github.com/isislab-unisa/3Dify. We are excited to share our resources with the academic community and industry experts to foster further innovation and collaborations.

🌟 We look forward to continuing this exciting journey and sharing more updates with all of you soon!
