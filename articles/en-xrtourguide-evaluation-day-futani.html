📅 The XRTourGuide – FUTURAL Evaluation Day will take place on Wednesday, April 15, 2026 in Futani (SA), Italy.
A full day dedicated to exploring and testing innovative Extended Reality (XR) tools for enhancing cultural and environmental heritage 🌿
📌 On the agenda:
✅ XRTourGuide app presentation and testing
✅ Hands-on workshops and activities
✅ Immersive experiences based on developed tours
✅ Networking with experts, institutions, and local communities
🎯 A great opportunity to explore new paths in:
🌱 sustainable tourism
💻 digital innovation
📍 place-based promotion
👩‍🏫 Schools, students, and teachers are welcome (also within PCTO and orientation activities)
🤝 Local communities and associations are key actors of the project
