Update: Progress in Academic Research Project on 3D Avatar Generation 🔍

We're pleased to share an update on our ongoing academic research endeavor focused on 3D avatar generation.

A significant step forward has been the development of the backend architecture for our avatar generation pipeline. We opted for the Next.js framework to construct a solid foundation capable of supporting the complexities involved in generating semirealistic 3D avatars.

For those interested in the technical specifics, our backend repository can be found at:

https://github.com/isislab-unisa/3dify-cloud-native/

Follow along as we continue to explore and innovate in the realm of 3D avatar technology. More updates to come as our research progresses.
