Our team is thrilled to share the latest progress in our journey towards revolutionizing digital avatars. We've just completed the design of a brand-new user interface that promises to enhance the way users interact with and customize their avatars. 🌟

User-Friendly Preview and Customization Interface: Our new UI is designed with the user in mind, offering an intuitive and seamless experience.

Advanced Customization Features: Users can now modify a wide array of avatar characteristics, including:

  • Face Features: Fine-tune facial attributes to create a unique and personalized look.
  • Lighting Setup: Adjust lighting to achieve the perfect ambiance and mood.
  • Facial Animation: Bring avatars to life with customizable facial expressions and animations.

This milestone is a significant step forward in making avatar generation more accessible and tailored to individual preferences. We can't wait to see how our research will impact the SERMAS project. Stay tuned for more updates as we continue to innovate and push the boundaries of what's possible!
