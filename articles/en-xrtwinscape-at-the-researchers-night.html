On Saturday, October 11, at the Villa Comunale in Salerno, XRTwinScape took part in the UNISA Researchers’ Night – EU Project S.T.R.E.E.T.S., presenting interactive demos and activities.
It was a great opportunity to share with the public the value of scientific research and technological innovation, and to bring science closer to society.
Thanks to everyone who stopped by to explore our interactive demos! 🔬💡
