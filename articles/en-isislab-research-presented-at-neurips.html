The paper “Hypergraph Generation with Latent Diffusion” has been accepted at NeurIPS – Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems, one of the leading international conferences in the fields of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

The work is co-authored by Carmine Spagnuolo, Professor at the University of Salerno, and Valerio Di Pasquale, PhD and member of ISISLab.

The research addresses the problem of hypergraph generation using latent diffusion models, proposing an approach based on generative AI techniques for modeling and generating complex structures.

The paper contributes to the research activities carried out at ISISLab – University of Salerno in the areas of artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Authors: Carmine Spagnuolo, Valerio Di Pasquale

For further information about the conference: NeurIPS – Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems.
