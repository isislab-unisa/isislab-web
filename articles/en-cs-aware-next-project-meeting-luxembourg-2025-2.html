From June 4th to 6th, partners of the CS-AWARE-NEXT gathered in Luxembourg for an engaging three-day series of meetings, validation sessions, and networking activities. Hosted by Fujitsu, the event brought together key contributors to review progress, validate work packages, and plan the next phases of the project.

The week kicked off at the House of Startups with a Dissemination Event and a live WP4 validation session on incident decision-making and self-healing systems. A special “Lunch & Learn” offered insights into cross-WP collaboration and public engagement, followed by hands-on discussions, video creation, and pilot integration planning.

The second day continued at Fujitsu Luxembourg with validation sessions for WP1 and WP3, focusing on AI policy testing, anomaly detection, and contextual decision-making. The afternoon was dedicated to scenario planning and detailed WP status updates, wrapping up with a team dinner in the heart of Kirchberg.

On the final day, the General Assembly provided a platform to review the status and planning for WPs 3 to 7, with a strong emphasis on evaluation, dissemination, and the project's long-term outlook.
