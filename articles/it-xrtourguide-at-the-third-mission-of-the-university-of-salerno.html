The XRTourGuide project was one of the testimonials of the external ecosystem during the event dedicated to the Third Mission of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Salerno, a partner of the project 🎉

It was presented by Aldo Longo, president of the Mountain Community of Bussento – Lambro and Mingardo, and Mario Ugo Simonelli, project RUP 👥.

An opportunity to showcase how XR technologies can enhance the territory and to share ideas, insights, and future perspectives.
