A major achievement for the ISISLab research group: the CRESCENDO project has been funded by the Italian Ministry of University and Research under the PRIN 2026 programme, receiving a total grant of €1.2 million.

🌱 The growth of supercomputing and artificial intelligence is driving the need for increasingly powerful data centers, with a significant impact on energy consumption, water usage, and CO₂ emissions. CRESCENDO was created to address this challenge by developing innovative solutions to make advanced computing infrastructures more efficient and sustainable, from system software and data center operations to the assessment of their environmental, economic, and regional impact.

🎉 The project will be coordinated by Biagio Cosenza of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Salerno, in collaboration with Roberto Antonietti (University of Padua), Flavio Vella (University of Trento), William Fornaciari (Politecnico di Milano), and Stefano Cozzini (Area Science Park, Trieste).
