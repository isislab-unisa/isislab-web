🏛️ On May 9, 2026, the Ethnographic Museum of Morigerati hosted the testing event of the XRTourGuide project, an experience that brought together technology, culture, and local community.
📱 The event involved institutions, experts, and an international group of young volunteers, who tested the app and experienced an innovative immersive museum visit.
🤝 Representatives from the museum, the University of Salerno, and local institutions were in attendance.
🧪 The day highlighted the potential of immersive technologies for cultural heritage enhancement.
🥂 The event concluded with a moment of sharing and networking among participants.
