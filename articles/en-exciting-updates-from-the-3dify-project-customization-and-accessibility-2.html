We are thrilled to share the latest advancements from our 3Dify Project with the academic community! 👏

✨ We have developed an intuitive frontend that allows users to easily upload their image and download the generated avatar. This makes the avatar creation experience more accessible and user-friendly.

🎨 Additionally, we have added customization sliders for facial features of the avatar, enabling users to modify and adapt the avatar according to their preferences and style.

💡 These updates not only enhance the functionality of our tool but also allow users to explore and create unique, personalized avatars like never before!
