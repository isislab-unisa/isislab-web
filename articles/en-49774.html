MIS4TEL 2024 Workshop: TEL4FC - Technology-Enhanced Learning for Future Citizens

📅 26th-28th June, 2024 | Salamanca, Spain |

The TEL4FC workshop is meant to connect researchers, educators and technologists to promote interdisciplinary research around the role of Technology-Enhanced Learning in shaping future citizens that are fully aware of potentials and risks.

🗓 Important Dates:

Submission Deadline: March 15, 2024
