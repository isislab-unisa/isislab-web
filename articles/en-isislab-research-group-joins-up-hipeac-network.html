Biagio Cosenza is a new member of the HiPEAC network. The HiPEAC network (High Performance and Embedded Architecture and Compilation) - funded by the Computing Systems research sector of the FP7-ICT, the seventh framework program of the European Community - was initially formed in 2004. HiPEAC was created to organize the community of computer systems of Europe.
