We are proud to share that Gabriele Tuozzo Maria Angela Pellegrino and Antonio Lieto have won the Best Demo Audience Choice Award at the 24th International Semantic Web Conference (ISWC 2025) for their work on CHeCLOUD — the Cultural Heritage Linked Open Data Cloud.

CHeCLOUD offers a powerful and innovative way to explore and connect cultural heritage data through Linked Open Data technologies.
