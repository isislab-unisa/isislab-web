🎓 The first activity took place at the “Leonardo Da Vinci” Scientific High School in Vallo della Lucania, where students and teachers were involved in designing interactive digital tours to promote the local territory through immersive technologies.
💡 During the activities, students created content for the app, designed new routes, and collaborated with the technology partner Daniele Monaco (Picaresque) and the team.
🧪 The experimentation has already involved over 50 students.
🚀 The developed tours will soon be available on the XRTourGuide app.
