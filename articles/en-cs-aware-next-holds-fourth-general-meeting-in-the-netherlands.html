In June 2024, the fourth General Meeting of the CS-AWARE-NEXT project was held in The Hague, Netherlands. Focus Europe led the awareness-raising efforts for this initiative, which is set to conclude in June 2025. The project aims to enhance cybersecurity for local and regional organizations by implementing a "hybrid" cybersecurity system. During this phase, Focus Europe is managing Task 4.5, focusing on awareness initiatives to demonstrate the benefits and applications of the CS-AWARE-NEXT system.
