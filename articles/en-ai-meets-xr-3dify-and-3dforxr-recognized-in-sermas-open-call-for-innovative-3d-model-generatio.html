As part of the #SERMAS project, we at ISISLab – University of Salerno, in collaboration with Centro Regionale Information Communication Technology - CeRICT scrl , have developed 3Dify: a smart AI-powered tool that transforms a single photo into a fully rigged 3D avatar – ready for real-time use in XR, games, training, or education.
🧠 Built for non-experts, 3Dify uses cutting-edge AI to generate low-poly avatars with bone rigging and UVs. Export them in seconds and use them in Unity, Unreal Engine, Blender, or WebGL environments.
🎮 Perfect for:

  • XR content creators
  • Indie game developers
  • Educators and trainers
  • Digital media professionals
    ✨ We're proud to see 3Dify featured by DW News in a deep-dive article on XR/AI innovation — a great recognition of our work within the European research and tech community.
    👉 This tool was selected as one of the winning projects from the first SERMAS open call focused on XR/AI innovation.
