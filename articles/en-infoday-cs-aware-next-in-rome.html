On February 7, 2025, the CS-AWARE NEXT Infoday was held at the Italian Chamber of Deputies, bringing together institutions, cybersecurity experts, and local authorities to discuss digital security and data protection. The event featured project presentations, strategies for enhancing cybersecurity in local administrations, and a debate highlighting Focus Europe’s role in supporting effective digital protection, concluding with networking opportunities.
