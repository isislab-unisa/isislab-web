Congratulations to Professor Vittorio Scarano for accepting the research project Erasmus+ 2023: NEOLAiA which has finally been approved and funded by the European Commission and will receive a budget of 14.4 M€ (https://neolaiacampus.eu) for the next 4 years.

The University of Salerno was one of the 4 Italian universities selected in the latest call, Ersamus+ 2023 call, and among over 50 European alliances.
