On March 28, 2025, the Province of Matera hosted an Info Day for the CS-AWARE-NEXT project, focusing on enhancing cybersecurity in local governments. The event featured presentations on the project's objectives and tools, followed by discussions on implementing effective cybersecurity strategies in public administrations. This initiative is part of a series of outreach activities aimed at improving digital resilience across Italian municipalities.
