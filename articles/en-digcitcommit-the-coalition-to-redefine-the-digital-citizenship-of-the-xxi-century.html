The network of associations involved in redefining the digital skills of the citizens of the 21st century is born: "be safe, solve problems and become a force for good in the digital world". Among the partners, in addition to ISTE: Google, Education Facebook, Common Sense Education, KQED, Digital Citizenship Institute, and many more are joining. More info, full resources and the complete challenge on the DigCitCommit website.
