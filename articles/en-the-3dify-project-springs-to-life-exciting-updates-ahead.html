🎉 We're thrilled to announce that we've successfully surpassed our KPI on facial features, going from inferring 15 to 26 features!

The 3Dify project, a tool for automatic avatar generation from a single photo, continues to push boundaries in creating semi-realistic and lifelike avatars. Thanks to the excellent work of our team, our efforts have significantly enhanced the algorithm for face landmark points extraction, making avatars even more akin to the original photo. Stay tuned for more updates!
