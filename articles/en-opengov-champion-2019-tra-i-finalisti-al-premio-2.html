The " Open Data Campania " project, "coordinated by the regional Open Data Team and in collaboration with the research group (REA- Research Executive Agency for Horizon 2020, University of Salerno, Department of Computer Science, Prof. Vittorio Scarano), with subsequent contributions from hi-tech companies (Almaviva Group) and companies specializing in innovative services for the PA ( ForumPA) is one of the finalists for the "OpenGov Champion 2019" award for the "Transparency and Open data" category.
