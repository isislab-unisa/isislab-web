## Description

The **LEAD (Technology-enhanced Learning and Problem-solving Discussions: Networked Learning Environments in the Classroom)** project focuses on a specific type of cognitive learning activity based on collaborative problem-solving.

Its main objective is to develop a software system capable of supporting *face-to-face* discussions, fostering learning and knowledge construction not only through the use of computers, but also through collaboration and interaction among groups.

Within this context, the unit of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Salerno, coordinated by Vittorio Scarano, contributed to the development of techniques, technologies and methodologies for synchronous *face-to-face* cooperation.

The main outcome was **CoFFEE (Cooperative Face2Face Educational Environment)**, an extensible and programmable platform for developing cooperative systems, released under an open-source licence for the scientific community and educators.
