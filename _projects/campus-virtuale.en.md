## Description

The multidisciplinary project **Virtual Campus – From the Territory to Virtual Reality: The Fisciano Campus in Its Context** aims to develop virtual environments representing the University of Salerno campus and relevant locations within its geographical, historical and archaeological context.

The environments are designed to be accessed both locally and remotely, supporting real-time collaborative activities.

The project involves researchers from four departments of the University of Salerno:

- Department of Computer Science and Applications;
- Department of Education Sciences;
- Department of Cultural Heritage;
- Department of Environmental and Territorial Studies.
