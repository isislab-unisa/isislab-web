## Objectives

The PAUN project (Urban Archaeological Park of Naples), developed within the framework of the DATABENC High Technology District for Cultural Heritage, lays the foundations for the creation of an Urban Archaeological Park in Naples, initially focusing on the archaeological site of Piazza Municipio.

The project is based on the principle that knowledge is essential for the long-term enhancement of cultural heritage and that protection is a fundamental condition for passing it on to future generations.

Its objective is to improve access to information and services related to cultural heritage by promoting innovative solutions for the protection, enhancement and use of archaeological, monumental and environmental heritage.

## Activities and Technologies

The project is structured around three interconnected lines of action:

- **Integrated Knowledge System:** analysis of archaeological, monumental and environmental heritage, aimed at creating an integrated knowledge system within the CHIS platform.
- **Risk Monitoring:** development of a network for monitoring archaeological and historical-monumental risks, as well as the impact of tourist flows.
- **Social and Sustainable Visitor Experience:** development of tools and solutions based on augmented and virtual reality to promote innovative ways of experiencing cultural heritage.

## Collaboration

The RIPA–PAUN research project (Intelligent Network of Archaeological Parks – Urban Archaeological Park of Naples) was developed within the framework of the DATABENC High Technology District for Cultural Heritage.
