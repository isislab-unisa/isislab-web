## Description

The project focuses on designing a system for sharing comments on cultural resources available on the World Wide Web (WWW).

The initiative aims to encourage shared access to cultural heritage through digital tools that allow users to interact with resources and share observations and contributions.

## Funding

Regional Law No. 5/2002 project. Regional funding, requested in 2008 and awarded in 2015.
