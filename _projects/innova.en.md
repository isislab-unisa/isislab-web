## Description

**INNOVA – Regional Competence Centre for the Development and Transfer of Innovation in Cultural and Environmental Heritage**, funded through the Campania Regional Operational Programme, aims to establish a network integrating and strengthening research groups from the different scientific and technical fields involved in innovation for cultural and environmental heritage.

Among the activities coordinated by Vittorio Scarano, the project focuses on the study and application of technologies and methodologies for designing 3D environments.

The objective is to develop three-dimensional representations associated with cultural resources, accessible both locally and remotely through devices with different capabilities.
