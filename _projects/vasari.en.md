## Description

The **TOCAI – Knowledge-Oriented Technologies for Business Networking on the Internet** project, funded by MIUR, aims to study and develop innovative solutions that enhance the value of territories and their cultural and landscape heritage through the use of new technologies.

Its main objectives include the study and prototyping of an innovative ICT architecture based on cloud computing, mobile technologies and web applications. By also adopting the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigm, the architecture aims to provide intelligence to specific territorial contexts.

Given the wide range of application scenarios supported by the CHIS platform, the project also addresses the definition of the requirements that a space must meet to be considered *smart*, translating them into implementation specifications for the platform.
