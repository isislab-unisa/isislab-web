## Description

The **TOCAI project – Knowledge-oriented Technologies for Business Clustering on the Internet**, funded by MIUR (Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research), aims to investigate and develop innovative solutions for enhancing local territories and their cultural and landscape heritage through the use of new technologies.

One of its main objectives is to study and prototype an innovative ICT architecture based on cloud computing, mobile technologies and web applications. By also leveraging the Internet of Things (IoT) paradigm, this architecture is intended to provide intelligence to a specific territorial context.

Given the diversity of application scenarios supported by the CHIS (Cultural Heritage Information System) platform, the project also involves defining the requirements that a given area must meet to be considered *smart*, and translating these requirements into specifications for the implementation of the platform.
