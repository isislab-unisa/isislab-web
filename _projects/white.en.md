## Description

**WHITE – Web for Handicap Integrated Training Environment** is a project funded by the European Union under the Leonardo da Vinci programme.

The project focuses on developing software systems to improve educational and training environments, providing support to people with physical disabilities, particularly visual impairments.

Its objective is to contribute to the development of a software system offering advanced services to make web browsing more accessible and inclusive, overcoming barriers related to motor and visual disabilities, low vision, colour blindness and cognitive difficulties.
