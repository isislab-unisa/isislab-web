  XRTwinScape addresses a fundamental challenge in workforce training:
  preparing workers effectively in realistic environments without the
  risks and limitations associated with the use of physical spaces.
  Although training in real workplaces provides familiarity and practical
  experience, it also presents significant drawbacks, including safety
  concerns, the difficulty of replicating the same environment elsewhere,
  and limitations in training multiple people simultaneously.

  XRTwinScape proposes an innovative solution based on digital twins,
  highly detailed, full-scale virtual representations of physical
  workplaces, combined with immersive training lessons that enable
  contextual learning. By leveraging advanced technologies such as
  Gaussian Splatting, a non-conventional AI-based rendering technique
  that preserves exceptional visual detail, XRTwinScape enables the rapid
  creation of high-fidelity digital twins.

  The consortium brings together the expertise of four organisations.

  educational capabilities.

## Objectives

XRTwinScape aims to improve vocational training for Industry 5.0 through immersive virtual environments and high-fidelity digital twins.

The project enables workers to learn and practise operational procedures in realistic settings, reducing the risks and limitations associated with training in physical workplaces.

## Technologies and activities

- Creation of full-scale digital twins of working environments.
- Development of immersive and contextualised training lessons.
- Use of Gaussian Splatting for high-fidelity digital reconstruction.
- Application of XR technologies and Artificial Intelligence.
- Experimentation with new approaches to vocational training for Industry 5.0.

## Collaboration

The project brings together four organisations with complementary expertise:

- **FMTS:** vocational training and training activities at the Eduwork facility.
- **Eduwork:** a 2,000 m² industrial training environment.
- **CERICT:** XR applications and advanced digital reconstruction.
- **PICA:** immersive virtual environments for cultural heritage and industrial contexts.
