## Description

The **Structured Collaboration by CoFFEE in Jazz** project, recipient of the IBM Jazz Innovation Award 2008, integrates the collaborative tools provided by CoFFEE into the IBM Jazz architecture.

Its objective is to provide advanced synchronous collaboration functionalities to support design teams, facilitating communication, coordination and structured collaboration throughout the design process.
