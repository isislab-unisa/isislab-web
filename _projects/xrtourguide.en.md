## Objectives

XRTourGuide aims to promote tourism in rural areas through the use
of digital and immersive technologies.

The project aims to:

- Enhance the cultural and tourism heritage of rural areas.
- Develop innovative visitor experiences based on Extended Reality.
- Support the discovery and exploration of places through digital tools.
- Contribute to the promotion and sustainability of the territories involved.

## Activities and Technologies

The project focuses on the use of Extended Reality technologies
to create digital experiences that support visitors in exploring
and discovering local territories.

The main activities include:

- Designing immersive experiences for tourism.
- Developing digital tools to enhance access to local heritage.
- Integrating informational and multimedia content.
- Experimenting with new ways of interacting with places
  and their cultural and tourism resources.

## Partnership

The project involves three organisations:

- **University of Salerno (Italy):** lead partner of the project.
- **PICARESQUE SRL (Aversa, Italy):** project partner.
- **Comunità Montana Bussento – Lambro e Mingardo (Futani, Italy):**
  territorial partner supporting the enhancement of rural areas.

## Results

The project aims to develop a digital solution to support tourism
promotion and the enhancement of rural territories through content
and experiences based on XR technologies.
