## Objectives

LIGATE aims to foster innovation in drug discovery and development by integrating Computer-Aided Drug Design (CADD) techniques with the computing capabilities of Exascale supercomputers.

Its objective is to accelerate the identification of new treatments against viral and bacterial infections, including those caused by drug-resistant pathogens.

## Activities and Technologies

The project integrates and co-designs CADD tools and solutions, leveraging large-scale computational simulations and high-performance computing architectures.

Activities include the optimization of applications for heterogeneous parallel architectures equipped with accelerators, with particular attention to scalability on large HPC supercomputers and the use of modern programming models and runtime systems.

## University of Salerno Contribution

The University of Salerno contributes by accelerating CADD solutions on heterogeneous parallel architectures equipped with accelerators and optimizing their scalability on large-scale HPC infrastructures.
