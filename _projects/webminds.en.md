## Description

The **WEBMINDS – Wide-scalE, Broadband, MIddleware for Network Distributed Services** project, funded by MIUR, aims to study and develop a middleware infrastructure providing secure, reliable and continuously available access to information from both fixed and mobile devices.

The effectiveness of the infrastructure is demonstrated through applications for data access and sharing, as well as multimedia content delivery.

The architecture consists of multiple autonomous, geographically distributed servers and proxies that cooperate, including through the peer-to-peer paradigm. The infrastructure supports data storage, access and updating services in a scalable and easily reconfigurable way, according to changing requirements.
