## Research projects

Current and recent projects include XRTwinScape (immersive digital twins for training), XRTourGuide (extended reality for cultural discovery), CROMO (crowdsourced cultural heritage monitoring), NEOLAiA, LibreRT and CS-AWARE-NEXT.

Previous initiatives include SERMAS, LIGATE, VASARI, ROUTE-TO-PA and SNECS. Explore the official projects page for funding arrangements, institutions and dates.
