<p>The work-based learning activities were carried out in collaboration with the <strong>HETOR</strong> project, a pilot initiative of <strong>Route-To-PA</strong> (Raising Open and User-friendly Transparency-Enabling Technologies for Public Administrations). The project aims to encourage students to co-create Open Data about Cultural Heritage through the use of <strong>SPOD</strong>, the social platform for Open Data, in order to improve the quantity and quality of available data concerning the preservation and enhancement of local territorial resources.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>ProfAgri Salerno - Castel San Giorgio Campus</strong></h3>

<p>Students from the ProfAgri Institute in Castel San Giorgio created 5 datasets:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Regional Forests in Campania -</strong> This project aimed to identify the characteristics, owners, names, geolocation, wildlife, vegetation and many other details of the forests in the Campania region;</li>
<li><strong>Seed Forests in Campania -</strong> Census of seed forests in the Campania region, based on the file prepared by the Campania Region;</li>
<li><strong>Social Farms in Campania -</strong> Collection of all information concerning the characteristics of social farms in the Campania region, based on the file prepared by the Campania Region;</li>
<li><strong>Slow Food Presidia in Campania -</strong> Census of Slow Food products in Campania, based on the Slow Food Foundation's website. The analysis and data collection aimed to identify local traditional products. The listed products come from various provinces of Campania, each with its own distinctive characteristics;</li>
<li><strong>Farm Stays in the Province of Salerno -</strong> Census of farm stays in the city of Salerno and the surrounding province. The analysis and data collection, based on the file prepared by the Campania Region, aimed to identify the main characteristics generally required of a farm stay.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>A. Diaz State Scientific High School - San Nicola La Strada</strong></h3>

Maria della Pietà -</strong> Census of all decorative and furnishing elements in the three main churches of San Nicola La Strada: S. Maria delle Grazie, S. Maria degli Angeli and S. <h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Academic Year 2016–2017</strong></h2>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Open Data Co-creation</strong></h4>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>P.S. Mancini Scientific High School</strong> <strong>- Avellino</strong></h3>

<p>Students from the P.S. Mancini Institute in Avellino created 4 datasets:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Trademarks in the Province of Avellino -</strong> The dataset is based on information made available as Open Data by the Central State Archives in the "Trademarks" section. The research focused on companies in the province of Avellino that registered trademarks between 1897 and 1963;</li>
<li><strong>Central Political Register of the Province of Avellino -</strong> The dataset is based on information made available as Open Data by the Central State Archives under the heading "Central Political Register". The research focused on individuals from the province of Avellino who were under political surveillance from the second half of the nineteenth century until the aftermath of the Second World War;</li>
<li><strong>Irpino Museum of Avellino -</strong> This dataset was created in collaboration with the Irpino Museum of Avellino to enhance the museum's collections. The provincial Irpino Museum is located on Corso Europa. It was established in the second half of the nineteenth century following the bequest of lawyer Giuseppe Zingarelli's antiquities collection to the Municipality of Avellino;</li>
<li><strong>Irpino Museum: Epigraphs Section -</strong> This dataset was created in collaboration with the Irpino Museum of Avellino to better promote the section dedicated to the epigraphs held in the museum.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>School Code:</strong> AVPS12000T</p>

<p><strong>Datasets created:</strong> 4</p>

<li><strong>Abandoned Factories in Nocera Inferiore -</strong> This dataset aims to give new generations an insight into the city of Nocera Inferiore by highlighting its industrial past. It presents a historical and industrial archaeology route that invites people to imagine the city in a different era, when factories stood out with their buildings, many of which have since been demolished or repurposed;</li>
<li><strong>Open Museum of the G.B. Vico High School in Nocera Inferiore -</strong> The dataset was created to catalogue, analyse and make known the educational materials held at the G.B. Vico Classical High School.</li>
