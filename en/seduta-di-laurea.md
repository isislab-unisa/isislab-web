<p>The thesis procedure must be initiated <strong>no later than 60 days before</strong> the graduation session. Therefore, <strong>you must notify your contact person of your intention to participate in the graduation session more than 60 days in advance</strong>.</p>
<p>Once the graduation session you intend to attend has been agreed upon with both the professor and your contact person, you can proceed with the procedures required for the final examination. All relevant information is available on the following pages.</p>
<p><strong>UNISA Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science:</strong> <a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/didattica/esame-finale">https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/didattica/esame-finale</a>.</p>
<p><strong>UNISA Master's Degree in Computer Science:</strong> <a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica-magistrale/didattica/esame-finale">https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica-magistrale/didattica/esame-finale</a>.</p>
<p>The duly completed documents must be emailed to Dr Giusy Gonnella so that the supervising professor can sign them.</p>
<p><strong>Dr Giusy Gonnella's email address:</strong> <a href="/ggonnella-unisa-it/">ggonnella@unisa.it</a>.</p> </div>

<ul>
<li><strong>Thesis Proposal</strong> (online activation of the thesis procedure) – No later than 60 days before the graduation session.</li>
<li><strong>Application for Degree Conferral</strong> – Within 30 days before the graduation session.</li>
<li><strong>Attachments to the Application for Degree Conferral</strong> – Within 30 days before the graduation session.</li>
<li><strong>Final Thesis Attachments</strong> (Thesis and Abstract) – No later than 10 days before the graduation session.</li>
<li><strong>Supervisor's Approval</strong> – No later than 10 days before the graduation session.</li>
</ul>
