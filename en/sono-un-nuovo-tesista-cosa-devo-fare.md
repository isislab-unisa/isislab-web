<h2 class="wp-block-heading">When You Start Your Internship/Thesis in ISISLab</h2>

<p>The things to do as soon as you start working are:</p>

<ol>
  <li>request an account for the lab PCs (if you use Windows);</li>
  <li>request to be added to the mailing list.</li>
</ol>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Your Account on the Lab PC</h3>

<p>The account is needed to use the PCs the lab makes available. Usually you can pick any free PC and use it for your whole thesis work (you can switch PCs from time to time, but we advise against it, for convenience).</p>

<p>You can configure, install software on, and reformat “your” PC, with the authorization of a contact person.</p>

<p>Some tips and useful information:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Every time you turn the PC on or off, your home directory is transferred from/to the network storage. To speed things up, we recommend not overloading your user folder (Desktop, Documents, ...).</li>
  <li>Each account has a virtual drive Z: associated with it; use it to store your data.</li>
  <li>The Samba share <code>\\limbo\pub</code> is a shared directory accessible to everyone, used mainly to exchange files. Do not rely on this folder as an archive: it is the first thing we empty when we need space.</li>
  <li>If you need a Linux machine or need to install software, ask one of the contact people.</li>
  <li>At the end of each day, turn off the PC and the monitor.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Lab Mailing List</h3>

<p>The mailing list is the main tool we use to send notices of seminars (about once a week) or important and urgent communications (very, very rarely).</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">When You Give a Seminar</h2>

<p>About a week before the seminar, let Giuseppina Palmieri know the date, the time, and a short abstract of the seminar.</p>

<p>
  To plan your seminar, we recommend reading the
  <em><a href="{{ 'en/faq-seminari/' | relative_url }}">FAQ on seminars</a></em>,
  the <a href="{{ 'en/informazioni-utili/' | relative_url }}">tips on short seminars</a>,
  and anything else listed in <a href="{{ 'en/informazioni-utili/' | relative_url }}">Useful Information</a>.
</p>

<p>Remember to put all the information about the seminar on your personal page, including the outline of the topics covered, the links used, the bibliography, etc.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">When You Prepare for Your Graduation Session</h2>

<p>Typically you are asked to give a final (full) seminar on your work and, a few days before the thesis defense, a rehearsal of the thesis presentation. The presentation itself is short (5-6 minutes), so it must be prepared with care and attention.</p>

<p>Some <a href="{{ 'en/consigli-per-i-seminari-brevi/' | relative_url }}">tips on short seminars</a> can help you.</p>
