## Recognition and coverage

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">ROUTE-TO-PA Project</h2>

<p>The results of the ROUTE-TO-PA Project have been selected by the European Union for inclusion in the Innovation Radar on the <a href="http://www.innoradar.eu/" rel="noreferrer noopener">www.innoradar.eu</a> portal.</p>

<p>The Innovation Radar is a European Commission initiative to identify high-potential innovations and innovators in EU-funded research and innovation projects. Its goal is to enable all citizens, public officials, professionals and business people to discover the results of EU innovation funding and to give them the opportunity to search for innovators.</p>

<p>The project's innovations consist of:</p>

<ul>
  <li>A new Open Data Platform that increases transparency in Public Administration and offers a user-friendly, easily accessible environment for public administrators, organizations and citizens to access, reuse, create and publish open data (based on SPOD and TET). The entry is available on the Innovation Radar portal: <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873" rel="noreferrer noopener">https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873</a></li>
  <li>An open, browser-based software tool for creating and analyzing decision trees (SilverDecisions). The entry is available on the Innovation Radar portal: <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875" rel="noreferrer noopener">https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875</a></li>
</ul>

<p>The recognition of the project's results is very positive and acknowledges the value of the work that the whole team put in during and after the project!</p>
