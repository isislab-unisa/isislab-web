## Starting an internship

Open the [UNISA internships page](https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/attivita-e-servizi/tirocini) and find the section **“Tirocinio curriculare (interno)”**. Download both forms from that page and complete them as follows.

- **Laboratory:** Enter `ISISLab`.
- **Internship period:** Enter the maximum period of 12 months. Use the date on which you plan to submit your application as the start date and the date 12 months later as the end date.
- **Credits (CFU) and total hours:** Check your personal Esse3 account for the number of credits assigned to your internship and the corresponding number of hours.
- **Academic supervisor:** Enter the name of the professor supervising your internship.
- **Internship objectives and activities:** Agree on these with your internship contact before completing the forms.

Email both completed forms to [Dr Giusy Gonnella](mailto:ggonnella@unisa.it) so she can arrange for your academic supervisor to sign them.

Once your supervisor has signed both documents, email them to the Department of Computer Science Internship Office at [carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it](mailto:carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it). Use the following subject line, replacing `[Docente]` with your supervisor’s name and `[Studente]` with your name:

`[Docente] + [Studente] TI ProgForm e Addendum`

## Keeping your internship logbook

Update your logbook as you complete your internship activities. Recording dates and hours as you go makes it easier to spot discrepancies before submitting the logbook.

## Completing and validating your internship

When you finish your internship project, complete any remaining entries in your logbook. Before submitting it, check that:

1. The total hours recorded meet the requirements for your internship and the credits (CFU) assigned to it.
2. The dates recorded do not conflict with public holidays, classes, exams or other commitments.
3. You have signed every field that requires your signature.

We recommend sending the logbook to your internship contact for a final check. Then email the completed logbook to [Dr Giusy Gonnella](mailto:ggonnella@unisa.it), copying your academic supervisor so that their signature can be obtained.

Once your supervisor has signed, send the completed documents to the Department of Computer Science Internship Office at [carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it](mailto:carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it).
