<p>Information about starting an internal internship is available on the following page, under the “Curricular Internship (Internal)” section.</p>
<p><strong>UNISA Internships:</strong> <a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/attivita-e-servizi/tirocini">https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/attivita-e-servizi/tirocini</a>.</p>
<p>The forms to be completed can be downloaded from the following page:</p>
<p><strong>UNISA Forms:</strong> <a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/modulistica">https://corsi.unisa.it/informatica/modulistica</a>.</p>
<p>The first required document is called “<a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/uploads/rescue/499/3520/fac-simile-progetto-formativo-tirocinio-interno.docx">Internal Internship Training Project Form (Template)</a>”.</p>
<p>The second required document is called “<a href="https://corsi.unisa.it/uploads/rescue/499/3520/addendum-interni-distanza.docx">Addendum for Remote Internal Internships – Phase 3</a>”.</p>
<p>Both documents must be completed in full. The following instructions explain what information should be provided:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Laboratory:</strong> ISISLab.</li>
<li><strong>Internship period:</strong> always indicate the maximum duration of 12 months, starting from the date on which you intend to submit the application and ending 12 months later.</li>
<li><strong>ECTS credits and total hours:</strong> check the number of ECTS credits and the corresponding required hours in your personal Esse3 account.</li>
<li><strong>Academic supervisor:</strong> the professor in charge of supervising the internship.</li>
<li><strong>Internship objectives and arrangements:</strong> to be agreed upon with your contact person.</li>
</ul>
<p>Once completed by the student, both documents must be emailed to Dr Giusy Gonnella so that the professor in charge, indicated in the documents as the academic supervisor, can sign them.</p>
<p>The fully completed documents, bearing the academic supervisor's signature, must then be emailed to the Internship Office of the Department of Computer Science, using the following email subject: “ [Lecturer] + [Student] TI ProgForm and Addendum ”.</p>
<p><strong>Dr Giusy Gonnella's email address:</strong> <a href="mailto:ggonnella@unisa.it">ggonnella@unisa.it</a></p>
<p><strong>Internship Office email address:</strong> <a href="mailto:carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it">carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it</a>.</p>

<p>Once the project included in the internship has been completed, the internship logbook must be filled in, if this has not already been done. Make sure that:</p>
<ol>
<li>the total hours recorded meet the number of hours and ECTS credits required for your internship;</li>
<li>the dates entered do not conflict with other events (public holidays, courses, exams, etc.);</li>
<li>you have signed all the fields where a signature is required.</li>
</ol>
<p>Once these checks have been completed, it is advisable to send the logbook to your contact person for an additional review.</p>
<p>The duly completed logbook must be emailed to Dr Giusy Gonnella, with your academic supervisor in cc, so that it can be signed.</p>
<p>The fully completed documents, bearing the academic supervisor's signature, must then be sent to the Internship Office of the Department of Computer Science.</p>
<p><strong>Dr Giusy Gonnella's email address:</strong> <a href="mailto:ggonnella@unisa.it">ggonnella@unisa.it</a></p>
<p><strong>Internship Office email address:</strong> <a href="mailto:carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it">carrierestudenti.di@unisa.it</a>.</p>
