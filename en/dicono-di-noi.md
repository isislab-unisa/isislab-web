## Recognition and coverage

The European Commission’s Innovation Radar has recognized results from the ROUTE-TO-PA project, which coordinated efforts around transparent and participatory use of open data.

Among the highlighted outputs are the SPOD/TET-based platform for creating, publishing and reusing open data and SilverDecisions, a browser-based tool for decision trees.

Read the Innovation Radar profiles linked below.

**Innovation Radar:** [Open data platform](https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873) · [SilverDecisions](https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875).
