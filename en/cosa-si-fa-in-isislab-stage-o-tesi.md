<p>This lab carries out research and teaching activities.</p>

<p>As for research, see ISISLab's <a href="{{ '/en/research-area/' | relative_url }}">Research Topics</a>. You can also browse the list of our <a href="{{ '/en/scientific-publications/' | relative_url }}">scientific publications</a>.</p>

<p>As for teaching, ISISLab hosts theses for the pre-reform (old-system) degree and for the Master's degree, as well as internships as a training activity for the Bachelor's degree. The topics of our theses closely follow the interests of the lab, namely distributed and parallel computing and interactive graphics. More details are best obtained in person… Contact us!</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">If you are a student considering an internship or a thesis in ISISLab…</h2>

<ul>
<li>Great! Before we meet in private, you might want to come along to one of our seminars, to get a feel for the atmosphere of the lab and perhaps hear something that interests you.</li>

<li>As you will have heard from your fellow students, internship/thesis work is carried out in the lab, since close contact with us at ISISLab and with your fellow interns/thesis students is essential;</li>

<li>As you can see, all your colleagues give seminars regularly (one is held every week). The purposes are many: first of all, it lets all of you know what your colleagues are working on; then, it gives you the chance to gain experience in presenting your work (which will come in handy at your first job interviews); finally, it allows us to assess the progress of your thesis work collectively.</li>

<li>In short, we work together, and productively. Come and see us!</li>

<li>If you want more information, see “<a href="{{ 'en/sono-un-nuovo-tesista-cosa-devo-fare/' | relative_url }}">I'm a new thesis student: what do I do now?</a>” and also the <a href="{{ 'en/informazioni-utili/' | relative_url }}">Resources for thesis students</a> page.</li>
</ul>
