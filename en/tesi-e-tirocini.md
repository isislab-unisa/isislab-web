  <h2>What We Do in ISISLab: Internships and Theses</h2>
  <p>The lab offers students the opportunity to carry out internships, placements, and bachelor's and master's theses, taking an active part in research activities and working closely with researchers and other students.

  The topics range from distributed and parallel systems to the Web, from open data to the enhancement of cultural heritage, up to interactive graphics and immersive technologies. Work in the lab is supported by regular discussion sessions and seminars, which are useful for sharing results, exploring new ideas, and developing scientific and communication skills.

  An experience that lets you put into practice the knowledge gained during your studies, contribute to real projects, and get closer to the world of research.</p>
