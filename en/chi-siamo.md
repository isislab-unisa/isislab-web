## About ISISLab

ISISLab has worked at the University of Salerno since 1995, bringing together research in distributed and parallel computing, collaborative systems, and immersive visual interaction.

Research topics include cloud and edge computing, large-scale simulation, open data, privacy-aware collaboration, visualization and virtual reality. The lab has contributed to more than twenty national and European projects, including coordinating the H2020 ROUTE-TO-PA project.

Meet the research community, explore current projects and discover opportunities to collaborate.
