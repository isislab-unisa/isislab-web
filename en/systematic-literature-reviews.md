## Systematic Literature Reviews

A systematic literature review helps identify, screen and synthesize publications using a documented search protocol.

Workflows normally involve defining research questions, bibliographic search, inclusion and exclusion criteria, data extraction and quality assessment. The ISISLab resource page provides related materials.
