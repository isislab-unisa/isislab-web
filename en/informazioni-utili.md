<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Research Projects and Thesis Work Available on GitHub</h3>

<p>The ISISLab GitHub repository collects the research projects and theses developed by the students and the team of the lab at the University of Salerno. Here you can find some of the main research and thesis projects carried out by researchers and students of the ISISLab laboratory of the Department of Computer Science of the University of Salerno. Each repository tells of concrete experiences and results in innovative areas such as cloud computing, XR, and knowledge graphs, among others.</p>
