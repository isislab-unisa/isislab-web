## Seminars at ISISLab

Seminars provide students working on their theses and researchers with an opportunity to exchange ideas on technologies, methods, experiments and research findings. Presentations can accompany the different stages of thesis work.

### Event – 9 October 2026

The ISISLab public homepage announced a meeting dedicated to the **Sail Platform**, a web platform for managing and monitoring sailing regattas, featuring **Davide Petracca** and **Armando Pio La Rocca**, as well as a presentation of **Anna Chiara Esposito's** thesis work.

Venue: ISISLab Laboratory, Building F, Stecca 7, 2nd floor, Fisciano Campus.
