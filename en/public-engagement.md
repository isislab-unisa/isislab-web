<p>ISISLab is actively involved in communicating and sharing digital knowledge with society and the local community. To this end, it regularly organises activities in collaboration with various volunteer associations to promote topics of particular interest.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Questions and Answers: Open Science and Open Data (25/03/2019)</h4>

<p>In collaboration with the <a href="http://www.mindthegaps.it/">Mind the Gaps</a> Association and the <a href="https://www.progettovivarium.it/">Vivarium Project – Digital Humanities</a>, Professor Vittorio Scarano gave an interactive presentation on Open Data and Open Science. The event was part of the activities organised for <strong>ALL DIGITAL Week 2019</strong> and took place on 25 March 2019 at 5:30 p.m. in Room P6 of the Department of Computer Science at the University of Salerno.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading">Knowledge Loves Wikipedia (26/03/2019)</h4>

<p>Also as part of the European campaign <strong>ALL DIGITAL Week 2019</strong>, Professor Delfina Malandrino, in collaboration with the <a href="http://www.mindthegaps.it/">Mind the Gaps</a> Association, the Department of Computer Science at the <a href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universit%C3%A0_degli_Studi_di_Salerno">University of Salerno</a>, the <a href="https://www.progettovivarium.it/">Vivarium Project – Digital Humanities</a>, and <a href="https://www.wikimedia.it/">Wikimedia Italia</a>, organised an initiative aimed at promoting knowledge sharing and encouraging everyone to participate in the collaborative creation of knowledge. The event took place on 26 March 2019 at 2:00 p.m. in the laboratories of the Department of Computer Science.</p>
