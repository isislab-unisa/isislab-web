<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Bibliometrics</h1>

<p style="text-align: justify;">On this page, we collect information that may be useful to researchers for evaluating bibliometric indicators. Mostly links that we keep sharing with each other via email, Slack, or WhatsApp, and that we're trying to gather here! </p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bibliometric indicators</h2>

<p>When choosing the best venue for your own publication, <strong>after carefully considering the topics of the journal/conference</strong>, you should also look at the indicators used to evaluate it. Here are some links you can use.</p>

<ul>
<li>SCImago Journal and Country Rank (SJR) for Computer Science (International):
<ul>
<li><a aria-label="valutazione dei giornali (opens in a new tab)" href="https://www.scimagojr.com/journalrank.php?area=1700" rel="noreferrer noopener">evaluation of journals</a> with respect to your own area and subarea, including the H-index, the SJR ranking, and the trend over the years</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>SCOPUS:
<ul>
<li><a aria-label="valutazione dei journals (opens in a new tab)" href="https://www.scopus.com/sources?sortField=citescore&amp;sortDirection=desc&amp;isHiddenField=false&amp;field=subject&amp;subject=&amp;asjcs=17&amp;_openAccess=on&amp;_countCheck=on&amp;count=0&amp;countField=documentsMin&amp;_bestPercentile=on&amp;_quartile=on&amp;_quartile=on&amp;_quartile=on&amp;_quartile=on&amp;_type=on&amp;_type=on&amp;_type=on&amp;_type=on&amp;year=2018&amp;offset=&amp;resultsPerPage=20" rel="noreferrer noopener">valutazione dei journals</a> with respect to your own area and subarea, including the CiteScore and recent citations</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><a aria-label="List of journals (opens in a new tab)" href="http://www3.unich.it/vqranvur/Criteri%20GEV/allegati%201/Computer_Science.pdf" rel="noreferrer noopener">List of journals</a> of the GEV (for ANVUR's VQR 2004-2010) from the University of Chieti
<ul>
<li><a aria-label="from the University of Padua, with the report (opens in a new tab)" href="https://www.math.unipd.it/it/dipartimento/valutazione/VQR2004-2010_Area01_Appendici.pdf" rel="noreferrer noopener">from the University of Padua, with the report</a></li>
</ul>
</li>

<li>Conference rating (Italy (GRIN-GII) and Spain (SCIE)): <a href="http://gii-grin-scie-rating.scie.es/">http://gii-grin-scie-rating.scie.es/</a></li>
<li>GRIN guidelines on evaluation: <a href="https://grin-informatica.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/manifesto-grin-valutazione.pdf">https://grin-informatica.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/manifesto-grin-valutazione.pdf</a> </li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Personal bibliometric indicators</h2>

<p>In the evaluation of one's own research activities, bibliometric indicators on one's own publications are becoming increasingly important. Here are some tools you can use to assess yours!</p>

<ul>
<li>Scopus:  <a aria-label="author search (opens in a new tab)" href="https://www.scopus.com/search/form.uri#author" rel="noreferrer noopener">author search</a> </li>
<li>Web of Science: <a aria-label="author search (opens in a new tab)" href="http://wcs.webofknowledge.com/AF/AFHome.do?product=WOS&amp;editions=BSCI,BHCI,SCI,ESCI,AHCI,SSCI,ISSHP,ISTP&amp;endDate=null&amp;rurl=http%3A%2F%2Fapps.webofknowledge.com%2FWOS_GeneralSearch_input.do%3Fproduct%3DWOS%26search_mode%3DGeneralSearch%26preferencesSaved%3D%26SID%3DF1gZg5pJhDcOdxjcC9X%26PointOfEntry%3Dhome&amp;startDate=null&amp;SID=F1gZg5pJhDcOdxjcC9X" rel="noreferrer noopener">author search</a> </li>
<li><a aria-label="Google Scholar (opens in a new tab)" href="https://scholar.google.it/" rel="noreferrer noopener">Google Scholar</a></li>

</ul>
