<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Bibliometrics</h1>

<p style="text-align: justify;">On this page, we collect information that may be useful to researchers for evaluating bibliometric indicators. Mostly links that we keep sharing with each other via email, Slack, or WhatsApp, and that we're trying to gather here!</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Bibliometric indicators</h2>

<p>When choosing the best venue for your own publication, <strong>after carefully considering the topics of the journal/conference</strong>, you should also look at the indicators used to evaluate it. Here are some links you can use.</p>

<ul>
  <li>SCImago Journal and Country Rank (SJR) for Computer Science:
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://www.scimagojr.com/journalrank.php?area=1700">Computer science journal rankings</a> by subject area and category, including the H-index, SJR and trends over time.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li>Scopus:
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://www.scopus.com/sources">Browse journals by subject area</a> and compare CiteScore and recent citations.</li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li><a href="{{ '/assets/media/a36fc2b28cad3c-computer-science.pdf' | relative_url }}">Computer science journal list (PDF)</a> for the 2004–2010 VQR. See also the historical reference: Area 1 report, appendices (2004–2010).</li>
  <li>GII-GRIN-SCIE conference rating (Italy and Spain): <a href="https://scie.lcc.uma.es/">search the computer science conference ratings</a>.</li>
  <li><a href="https://www.grin-informatica.it/valutazione/">GRIN guidelines on research evaluation</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Personal bibliometric indicators</h2>

<p>In the evaluation of one's own research activities, bibliometric indicators on one's own publications are becoming increasingly important. Here are some tools you can use to assess yours!</p>

<ul>
  <li>Scopus: <a href="https://www.scopus.com/freelookup/form/author.uri">author search</a></li>
  <li>Web of Science: <a href="https://www.webofscience.com/wos/author/author-search">author search</a></li>
  <li><a href="https://scholar.google.it/">Google Scholar</a></li>
  <li><a href="https://dl.acm.org/">ACM Digital Library</a></li>
</ul>

<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Systematic Literature Reviews</h1>

<p style="text-align: justify;">On January 14, 2020, Vittorio Scarano gave two seminars for Computer Science PhD students on “Systematic Literature Reviews: Principles, Methods and Tools.” The slides are available on SlideShare: <a href="https://www.slideshare.net/VittorioScarano/1-systematic-literature-reviews-introduction-and-methods-220557586">1 – SLR: Principles and Methods</a> and <a href="https://www.slideshare.net/VittorioScarano/2-systematic-literature-reviews-tools">2 – SLR: Tools</a>. The references and other resources used in the seminars are collected below.</p>
