## European Innovation Radar

The ROUTE-TO-PA results on collaborative open-data platforms and SilverDecisions were identified by the European Commission’s Innovation Radar.

**Further recognition:** The CHeCLOUD demo by Gabriele Tuozzo, Maria Angela Pellegrino and Antonio Lieto received the Best Demo Audience Choice Award at ISWC 2025.
