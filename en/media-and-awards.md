  <h2 style="color: #245b85; margin-top: 0;">Recognition and coverage</h2>

  The European Commission’s Innovation Radar has recognized results from the ROUTE-TO-PA project, which coordinated efforts around transparent and participatory use of open data.

  Among the highlighted outputs are the SPOD/TET-based platform for creating, publishing and reusing open data and SilverDecisions, a browser-based tool for decision trees.

  Read the Innovation Radar profiles linked below.

  <p><strong>Innovation Radar:</strong></p>
  <ul>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open Data Platform</a>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SilverDecisions</a>
    </li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Best Awards</h3>
    <div class="wp-block-ninja-blocks-icon-list">
    <ul class="list-item">
      <li><strong>IBM Jazz Innovation Award 2008</strong>. The proposal by the research group of the ISISLab laboratory at the University of Salerno, which won the award, is based on the work that led to the development of the CoFFEE software - Cooperative Face2Face Educational Environment - and aims to provide the work team with a rich set of collaboration tools ("Threaded Discussion" and "Graphical Discussion") capable of fostering structured and effective collaboration.</li>

      <li> The Italian training award "<strong><em><a aria-label="Aldo Fabris" href="http://www.premiofabris.it/" rel="noreferrer noopener">Aldo Fabris</a></em></strong>" (7th Edition) for the LEAD project. The "Aldo Fabris" award for training and education is an Italian national recognition, established in 2002 by public organizations involved in training and education, including the city of Rome, the Puglia region and Formez (a government public administration agency for international dissemination). Each year, awards are given in four categories: private sector, public sector, universities and schools. LEAD received the first prize for 2008 in the university category.</li>
    </ul>
      </div>

    <h3>Innovation Awards</h3>
    <div class="wp-block-ninja-blocks-icon-list">
      <p>
        The results of the ROUTE-TO-PA project were selected by the European Commission for inclusion in the Innovation Radar (www.innoradar.eu). The Innovation Radar is a European Commission initiative aimed at identifying high-potential innovations and their innovators within EU-funded research and innovation projects. The overall goal of the Innovation Radar is to enable every citizen, public official, professional or entrepreneur to discover the results of EU funding for innovation and to get in touch with innovators. The recognized innovations are:
      </p>

      <ol>
        <li>
          A new open data platform designed to improve the transparency of Public Administration and offer an intuitive and accessible environment – intended for public administrators, organizations and citizens – to access, reuse and publish open data (based on SPOD and TET).
          Visit the Innovation Radar:
          <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29873" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Open data platform</a>.
        </li>
        <li>
          An open source, browser-based software tool for creating and analyzing decision trees, developed starting from SilverDecisions.
          Visit the Innovation Radar:
          <a href="https://www.innoradar.eu/innovation/29875" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SilverDecisions</a>.
        </li>
      </ol>
    </div>
