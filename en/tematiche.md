## Research areas

Distributed and parallel computing covers cloud and edge systems, scalable architectures, massive simulation and high-performance computing.

Collaborative and social computing covers open-data co-creation, shared workspaces and privacy-aware systems. Visualization and interaction examines immersive experiences, scalable displays, innovative interfaces and virtual reality.
