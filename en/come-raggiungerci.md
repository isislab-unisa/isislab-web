## Directions and contacts

ISISLab is located in the Department of Computer Science, University of Salerno, Fisciano campus: Building F, Stecca 7, Laboratory 10, second floor.

Postal address: Via Giovanni Paolo II, 132, 84084 Fisciano (SA), Italy.

Check the university website for current campus access and transport directions.
