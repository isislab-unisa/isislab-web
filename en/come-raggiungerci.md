## How to reach ISISLab

ISISLab is located in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Salerno, Fisciano campus, Building F, Stecca 7, Laboratory 10, second floor.

The university's postal address is:

**Via Giovanni Paolo II, 132 – 84084 Fisciano (SA), Italy**

You can visit the [University of Salerno website](https://www.unisa.it/) or use [Google Maps to reach the campus](https://goo.gl/maps/CEZoKgS7TFH8qYiM6).

## Directions by car

### From Naples

Take the A30 Caserta–Salerno motorway towards Salerno. After passing Mercato San Severino, continue for approximately 4 km and take the Avellino exit (point 1).

Re-enter the motorway and continue until the Fisciano exit (point 2). At the exit, take the first right towards the University (point 3).

The dedicated lane leads directly to the university campus entrance (point 4). Parking is available on both sides of the road (point 5).

Parking is free. If possible, we recommend using the parking area on the left. ISISLab is located in Building F, on the second floor.

### From Salerno

Take the motorway towards Avellino and leave at the Fisciano exit (point 2).

Please note: ignore the earlier University exit and continue for a few hundred metres. Keep right at the fork, following the signs for Avellino, and then take the Fisciano exit.

### From Avellino

Take the motorway towards Salerno and leave at the Fisciano exit.

At the exit, turn left. After crossing the overpass over the motorway, follow the directions described from point 3 in the route from Naples.

## Finding ISISLab

Once you enter the campus, head towards Building F, following the red arrow shown in the photographs.

Enter the building and walk along the corridor until you reach the double doors leading to the atrium, where you will find the stairs and the lift.

Go up to the second floor. Once there, follow the corridor on your left. ISISLab is located in the first two rooms on the right.
