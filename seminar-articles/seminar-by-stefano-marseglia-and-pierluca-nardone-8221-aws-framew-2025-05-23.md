• Abstract :
Over the years we’ve become more and more familiar with the term “serverless application” and its main benefits such as cutting down costs and reducing infrastructure management overhead. But the implications of this new paradigm on the daily job of a developer are often somewhat overlooked. What does it really mean for a developer to work with serverless applications? In this talk we will give an answer to this question by presenting two AWS frameworks which support serverless application development on AWS: AWS SAM and AWS Amplify. During the discussion we will focus on what kind of issues these two frameworks try to resolve and will show their main features by means of some practical examples.
